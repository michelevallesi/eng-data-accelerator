# Users and stakeholders

The platform serves two populations that must not be confused: those who work on engineering data, and those who keep the integration running.

| Profile | What they look for | What they get | Where they interact |
| ----------- | ----------------- | --------------------- | ----------- |
| Systems or product engineer | linking and finding artefacts without switching tools | links created from their own tool, upstream and downstream impact | delegated dialog, side panel |
| Quality and compliance lead | coverage, coherence, evidence | matrices, maturity misalignments, dossiers with provenance | cockpit, reports |
| Technical project manager | programme state at milestones | reproducible snapshot at a baseline and baseline comparison | cockpit in *as of* mode |
| Configuration manager | coherent baselines across domains | coordinated baseline, observable Change, open dispositions | cockpit, PLM backbone |
| Auditor or certification body | state at a past date and its integrity | reconstruction from events, exclusion of AI-generated content | exported reports |
| Delivery team integrator | bringing a new tool into scope | generated and tested adapters, mappings and shapes | configuration console, repository |
| Platform administrator | stable operations | adapter health, error queue, reconciliation | administration console |
| Data steward and semantic curator | correct vocabularies and units | alignment proposals to approve four-eyes | configuration console |
