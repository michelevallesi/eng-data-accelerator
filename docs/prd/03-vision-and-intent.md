# Vision and intent

The vision is a **governed digital thread**: every engineering artefact — a requirement, a function, a product revision, a calculation case, a test — is reachable from any linked artefact, with its state, maturity and history, without anyone having to copy it or rebuild it by hand.

## Three roles, no others

| Role | What it means | What it does not mean |
| --- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Connect | stable identity, links navigable inside working tools, configuration context | copying data or replacing vendors' native integrations |
| Remember | the state of relationships at every baseline, rebuildable from events | becoming an archive or a system of record |
| Enforce | authority, maturity and configuration rules applied deterministically | approving, releasing or deciding in people's place |

## How to tell it works

- An engineer creates a link from their own tool without opening another, and the link stays valid when the artefact is renamed or revised.
- A cross-cutting impact question gets a complete answer in minutes, with an explicit statement of what is not up to date.
- A design review starts from numbers that can be opened, not from slides prepared by hand.
- An auditor gets the state at a past date exactly as it was, with proof that no model touched it.
- A new tool enters scope in under twenty person-days.

## Position relative to existing systems

The accelerator does not compete with systems of record: it presupposes them. The PLM backbone remains the product, configuration and Change anchor; the requirements platform and MBSE remain authorities for their own content; specialist tools remain authors of their own results. The accelerator occupies the space between them — the integration layer and the data hub — that none of them covers for other vendors' tools.

## Scope

- **In scope:** cross-tool identity and links, change propagation, cross-cutting projection and querying, quantity dictionary, maturity, publication and promotion of results, cockpit, governance of data and agents.
- **Out of scope:** authoring, running calculations, geometry management, the approval workflows of systems of record, legal records management.
